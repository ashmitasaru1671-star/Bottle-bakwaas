Bottle Bakwaas

Add everyone, put the phone in the middle, spin the bottle. No skipping! (Easier versions of exercise dares are fine.)

Auto-climb: Level 1 lasts 8 rounds, Level 2 lasts 20 rounds, then Level 3 till the end. No dare ever repeats. Your own dares come up early in their level.

How to play

Spin the bottle. Whoever it points at flips a card and does the dare. The group scores it. Refuse and you get a forfeit!
