# Dare Spin 🍾

A fast, interactive party game built for friends and family. Spin the bottle, flip your card, and complete the dare. 

> **Play it instantly in your browser (no downloads, no sign-ups):**  
> 🔗 **[https://ashmitasaru1671-star.github.io/Bottle-bakwaas/?v=4](https://ashmitasaru1671-star.github.io/Bottle-bakwaas/?v=4)**

---

### 🎮 How to Play
1. **Add your squad:** Enter everyone's name (works for any group size).
2. **Pick the vibe:** Choose Auto-climb mode or lock in a single difficulty level.
3. **Spin the bottle:** Watch it spin physics-style and land on a player's highlighted slice.
4. **Flip the card:** Complete your dare and let the group score your turn:
   - **Nailed it:** +2 points
   - **Okay:** +1 point
   - **Refused:** Get a funnier dare. Refuse twice and take a penalty (-1 point).
5. **Climb the levels:** Level 1 (Warm-up) ➔ Level 2 (Spicy) ➔ Level 3 (Total chaos).
